Privacy approach

Collect less. Explain more.

The website is designed to learn from basic interaction signals without needing sensitive conversation content or unnecessary personal data.

Website traces

What the optional trace server is intended to record.

Useful signalsPage views, join clicks, source-link clicks, session interest and other basic navigation events.
What it should not collectPasswords, private prompts, confidential work content, health records or unnecessary personal identifiers.
Why collect anything?To understand which questions resonate, which pages help and where visitors get lost.
External channelsWhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram and email have their own privacy terms when you choose to open them.
Community sharing

You control what you choose to share.

Do not share confidential company information or sensitive personal data in public or group discussions. Future evidence collection should be permission-based and clearly explain what is recorded, why, and how it may be reused.

Privacy by restraint

Useful learning should not require unnecessary surveillance.

Location and local circles

Local connection does not require precise-location tracking.

The current static website does not collect precise location. If local-circle discovery is introduced later, it should be opt-in and use only the minimum broad city/region information needed to help members find relevant gatherings. Private home addresses or precise live location should not be exposed as a community-discovery feature.
