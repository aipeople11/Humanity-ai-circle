Roadmap

Done → Now → Next → Future.

This is direction, not a promise of dates. Progress should follow real community behavior and evidence density.

DONE / FOUNDATION

What is in place

  • People-first thesis
  • Outcome-first tool philosophy
  • Responsible AI / human-control principles
  • Source-linked research framework
  • Community join channel
  • Recurring connection formats
  • Evidence maturity method
  • Multi-page website and exploration channels
NOW

What we are building through participation

  • Recurring global / topic conversations
  • Early local connection interest
  • Real questions from different life stages
  • First genuine member stories
  • Initial workflow/tool comparisons
  • Idea ↔ builder ↔ domain connections
  • Clear community norms and trust
NEXT

What should emerge next

  • Measured outcome-test pilots
  • Public evidence cards based on real runs
  • Contributor / tester recognition
  • Theme-based learning pathways
  • Better archive of shared learning
  • Repeat tests as models change
FUTURE / ONLY IF NEEDED

What may come later

  • Profession-specific circles when demand is real
  • Local city/region circles when genuine communities emerge
  • Collaboration / matching features
  • Private enterprise cohorts
  • Evidence APIs and research reports
  • Language / region expansion
  • Mentorship or opportunity layers only after organic demand
Decision rule

We do not add a feature just because an AI community could have it.

We add something when it helps people connect, adapt to AI, use it more effectively and responsibly, or turn shared learning into something useful.
