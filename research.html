How to read this research

Number → context → human meaning → question → source.

We use research to frame better conversations, not to pretend one statistic speaks for every person, profession or country.

1Check the populationWho was surveyed or modeled?
2Check the periodWhen was the underlying data collected?
3Check the scopeGlobal, sector-specific, profession-specific or modeled exposure?
4Translate carefullyWhat does the signal suggest — and what does it not prove?
5Open the sourceEvery research card links to the original publisher.
Research is a starting point. Community experience is labeled separately from documented runs, measured evidence and independent replication.
