Our rule

News becomes a human question, not a fear campaign.

When a major AI story appears, we ask: what does it mean for a person, what should we understand or test, and where should human judgment remain?

NEWS / SIGNAL→HUMAN QUESTION→DISCUSS / TEST→SHARE WHAT WE LEARN
