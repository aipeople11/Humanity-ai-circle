Recurring connections

The Circle keeps meeting.

Online. Local. Topic-based. Sometimes structured. Sometimes simply human. AI can answer questions; people still need people.

Technology should help us connect — not give us another reason to disconnect.

Humanity AI Circle creates reasons for people to keep talking to each other while AI becomes a bigger part of work, learning and daily life.

🌍Global conversationsOnline conversations across locations around meaningful AI questions, experiences and ideas.
📍Local circlesAs real member communities emerge in a city or region, people can meet nearby for conversation and learning.

Local, when it is real

A global community can become locally human.

As local communities emerge, members can help create Humanity AI Circle gatherings in their own cities or regions. We will not list a city as an active chapter until a real local community and gathering exist.

Future direction: members may eventually opt in to a broad city/region and interests so they can discover nearby conversations. The current website does not collect precise location.
