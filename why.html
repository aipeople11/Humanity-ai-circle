What are you trying to figure out?

💼

My job is changing

Understand what may change, what still needs human judgment, and what skills are worth building.

🧠

I need to upskill

Learn the durable skills: articulation, critical thinking, validation, workflow design and responsible use.

⚡

I want better productivity

Use AI where it helps — without adding more rework, complexity or unnecessary cost.

🧰

Too many tools

Start with the outcome, shortlist approaches, then choose the simplest reliable option.

🎓

Am I actually learning?

Use AI as support for understanding, not as a shortcut that replaces thinking.

✅

Can I trust this?

Learn to check sources, outputs, risks and limitations before acting on AI.

🤖

How much control should AI have?

Think about permissions, approvals, stop conditions and human override before autonomy.

❤️

What should stay human?

Keep relationships, responsibility, consequential judgment and human agency visible.
