Community promise

Trust is part of the product.

People learn better when they can ask honestly, disagree respectfully and understand the rules of the space they are entering.

Our promise

People first.

✓ No question is too basic
✓ Respect every experience level
✓ No unsolicited selling
✓ No paid praise disguised as evidence
✓ Disclose sponsorship or conflicts when relevant
✓ Protect confidential company information
✓ Separate experience from measured evidence
✓ Keep sources visible when making evidence claims
✓ Do not use fear as a substitute for understanding
✓ AI supports people — it does not replace human responsibility
Practical boundaries

Share what helps. Protect what should stay private.

Do not post passwords, confidential company data, regulated personal data or anything you are not authorized to share. Public discussions should stay respectful and useful. For high-stakes medical, legal, financial or safety decisions, community discussion is not a substitute for qualified professional advice.

A better discussion starts with better behavior

Ask honestly. Share carefully. Help responsibly.
